# CabecalhoApp

The top of the home screen: small logo on the left and the couple on the right.

- Left: the icon at 32px and "twise" in Bricolage Grotesque 26px.
- Right: the members' avatars, overlapping (initials, 36px).
  - The current user's avatar comes last, in `mint` with the letter in `on-mint`.
  - Tapping opens the workspace switcher and the profile.
- Below: the greeting in `display` and the period in `ink-muted`.
- On desktop, the logo moves to the top of the sidebar.
