# Illustrations (`ilustracoes`)

The brand's drawn language: brush and pen, two colors, on cream paper.

- **Colors (fixed):** `sketch-ink` stroke (#17191c), `mint` fill (#80dca5), and a `sketch-paper` background (#efebe0) built into each file (a rounded square). This keeps the illustration readable on any background, including the dark theme.
- **The stroke:** a brush line that thickens in the middle and thins at the ends, without closing perfectly. Large areas of solid black add weight (the body of the house, the wheels, the card's stripe).
- **The fill:** mint shifted slightly down and to the left of the stroke, like an off-register stamp, with an uneven edge.
- **The details:** dots in threes over the color areas, sparkle strokes (three strokes in a fan), looped curls. At most two kinds of detail per illustration.
- **Sizes:** 32px inside a 44px `sketch-paper` circle (category icon); 96–160px in empty states.
- **Accessibility:** decorative (`alt=""`) when the text next to it already says everything.
- **The set:** calendar (bills), card, wallet, house (housing), piggy bank (goals/savings), together (shared), leisure, groceries, coin (income), receipt (proof of payment), health, transport and travel. They are originals made for this system. An illustrator can complete the missing categories and the scenes with characters by following these rules.
