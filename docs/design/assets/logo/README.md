# Logo

The official twise logo files. Use them as they are, without redrawing.

- `twise-logo.svg`: full logo (icon + name), for light backgrounds (`bg-page`, `bg-surface`, `sketch-paper`).
- `twise-logo-claro.svg`: full logo with the name in off-white, for dark backgrounds.
- `twise-icone.svg`: the app icon, the owl on the mint square. It also serves as the social media avatar and the favicon.
- `twise-coruja.svg`: the owl with no background (mascot), always on `sketch-paper` or `mint-soft`.
- `twise-logotipo.svg` / `twise-logotipo-claro.svg`: the name only, in Bricolage Grotesque 750 converted to curves.

The colors are fixed: ink #17191c, mint #80dca5 and cream #efebe0. The idea behind the logo and the usage rules are in the brand guide (section **Brand: name, slogan and logo** in the design-system README, `docs/design/design-system/README.md`) and in the Logo component.
